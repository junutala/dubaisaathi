# Agreed design rules

Every rule here was asked for once. They are listed so that none has to be asked for twice, and
`design/check-screens.py` fails `npm run verify` for the ones a machine can check. When a new
rule is agreed, it is added here before the screen is drawn. Sprint 1, frozen 16 September.

| 2   | Red is reserved, and spent on one line only: जानना's emergency numbers above its tabs (rule 32, decision 046). It appears nowhere else. | 4   | The bar is on every screen after landing: four places evenly spaced, **each an icon with its word under it** — घर (`home`), दस्तावेज़ (`docs`), सुझाव (`bulb`), ऐप शेयर (`qr`), in that order (decision 046, replacing 027). घर is filled marigold and ऐप शेयर filled teal — two colours, never the same; दस्तावेज़ and सुझाव are plain. | 5   | The place the traveller is on is lit: a sand pill on a plain place, an inset ink ring on a filled one, and its word in bold. घर and its states light घर; घर.2–घर.3 light दस्तावेज़; घर.8 and घर.9 light their own; a pillar's screens light none — their header carries the pillar's colour.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | checker     |
| 6   | Home is the three pillars in that order, as deep blocks of their own hue with cream type, then बोलना (rule 14), with the pass tile under them (rule 13). | 14  | बोलना is घर's fourth block, after जानना — the same shape and type as the three, in its own plum — and it is on घर only while the phone has a signal, appearing and going with it, because everything behind it is online (decision 020, 17 September).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | checker     |
| 15  | घर never scrolls. | 17  | No cross, red cross or first-aid symbol on the documents tile, its screens or the app icon. | 18  | A permission the traveller refuses gets one screen that says what will not work and what still does, and nothing is asked again until they ask for it. ## Dropped, so they are not proposed again

- Voice input anywhere a traveller depends on it: the three pillars' boxes are typed into, and no
  offline recogniser ships (decision 016). बोलना is the one exception and is not a way back in —
  it is online, it is one block on घर, and it hands its sentence to a person, never to the matcher
  (decision 020).
- The Arabic phrase screens; the taxi card carries the one Arabic a driver needs.
- Hotels and homestays in जानना.
- Find-my-family, SOS to a contact in India, a settings screen, auto-deleting documents.
- A bar of icons without words (decision 027), and the pillars in the bar: since 28 September the
  bar is घर · दस्तावेज़ · सुझाव · ऐप शेयर with words, and the pillars are घर's blocks (decision 046).
- ज़रूरी जानकारी and its three capsules (decision 028): the documents are the bar's दस्तावेज़, the
  feedback is सुझाव, the numbers are जानना's red line (decision 046).
