तीन खंभे · The three pillars खाना · जाना · जानना Three verbs that rhyme, in the traveller's own language. They stay in Devanagari in both interface languages, with the Roman spelling underneath as a caption, so the names are the brand and never a translation.
खाना Khaana
कहाँ खाएँ — भारतीय खाना, छोटी दुकानें और कैफ़ेटेरिया। Where to eat. Indian dishes, the small eateries and cafeterias a tourist would never find. The dish is the search. The place is the answer.
जाना Jaana
कैसे जाएँ — मेट्रो, बस, ट्राम या टैक्सी, समय और दिरहम के साथ। How to get there. Metro, bus, tram or taxi, with time and fare, and the nearest stop. Type a place, or pick an attraction. Mistype it and we suggest the right one.
जानना Jaanna
क्या जानें — दुबई की जगहें, समय, टिकट, पहुँचने का तरीक़ा, हिंदी में। What to know. Attractions with hours, tickets and how to reach them, in Hindi. The lantern: light on a place before you go. Not hotels, not bookings.
Roman spelling Khaana · Jaana · Jaanna, with the double a. The single-a forms read wrongly in English: Jana is a name, Janna reads as an Arabic word, Khana is how nobody says it.
जाना and जानना One letter apart in both scripts, so the two never appear as bare words. Each carries its icon and its one-line blurb wherever it appears, including the bottom bar.
